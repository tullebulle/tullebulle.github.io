I'm a third year PhD student in Applied Mathematics at Harvard University. I work with Boris Kozinsky, Michael Albergo, and Yilun Du. I work on generative models with various applications including images, robotics and bio.

I did my undergrad in applied mathematics at the NTNU, where I under supervision of Espen Jakobsen wrote my thesis on fractional differential equations. During my undergrad, I spent a Fulbright year at MIT, where I worked with Yue Lu on asymptotics of high-dimensional statistical theory.
